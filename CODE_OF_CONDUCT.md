# Code of conduct

Keel is for people who build products together. Contributors and users of every background and
experience level are welcome. This applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and other spaces maintained for the project.

## How we work together

- Be respectful and specific. Critique an idea or implementation, not the person behind it.
- Make room for newcomers and different ways of communicating. Ask before assuming intent.
- Share evidence, explain trade-offs, and accept that maintainers may choose a different direction.
- Respect privacy, credit others' work, and honor requests to stop an interaction.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory attacks, sexual harassment, doxxing, impersonation, repeated
unwanted contact, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Neither are spam, malicious
contributions, or publishing private information without permission. Technical disagreement is
welcome; intimidation and personal abuse are not.

## Report a concern

Contact lead maintainer **Mohamed Alkiswani** at **[mk@getoya.ai](mailto:mk@getoya.ai)** privately.
Include the relevant links, what happened,
and how you would like to be contacted. Share only information needed to understand the report.
You do not need to confront the person involved or post a public accusation.

If the concern involves the contact or you are uncomfortable using that channel, use GitHub's
abuse-reporting tools or contact another repository maintainer privately when one is available.
Do not include private details in an issue just to request a reporting channel.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, give a warning, limit participation, or temporarily
or permanently remove access to project spaces. The response depends on the severity, impact,
and pattern of behavior. Serious threats or abuse can result in immediate removal.

Reports will be handled as privately as practical. Information may need to be shared with people
responsible for resolving the incident or with the hosting platform. Retaliation against someone
who raises a concern in good faith is itself a violation.

You may request clarification or appeal an action by emailing the contact above with relevant
context. A maintainer directly involved in a dispute should not be its sole reviewer when another
maintainer is available.

For software vulnerabilities, use the [security policy](SECURITY.md).
