Conductor is a Mac app.
Oya Factory is yours, everywhere.

Conductor made parallel agents feel polished on a Mac. Oya Factory brings the same workflow to macOS and Windows, open source and free, and adds proof: your checks on every turn, and agents that never collide.

Where Oya Factory goes further

  1. 01

    macOS and Windows

    One app, one workflow, on both platforms. Conductor is macOS only.

  2. 02

    Free and open source

    MIT licensed, no account and no paid tier. Read the code, change it, ship it.

  3. 03

    Your checks run on every turn

    After each turn, Oya Factory runs your project's own checks and records the verdict beside the reply. A turn that fails is never committed for you.

  4. 04

    Your code stays on your machine

    Everything runs locally; there is no cloud workspace and no server of ours between you and your repository.

  5. 05

    Projects configured for you

    A readiness scan, an agent team and the skills your stack needs, added and kept current automatically.
